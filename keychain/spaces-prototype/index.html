Dashboard

Simulator dashboard

Every control for the simulation lives here on the page — screens, display, theme, currency and the live feed. Changing anything here drives the mockup device; nothing here connects to real hardware.

Screens

Six screens is what fits comfortably in ESP32 firmware — everything else is a swipe away, nothing is buried in menus.

Authenticator

The keychain's personal authentication app — swipe to the last screen on the device. It shows time-based one-time (TOTP) 2FA codes for PaperStreet, GitHub and email, with a countdown bar to the next refresh. Tap an account on the device to enlarge its code, or hide all codes for privacy. These are demo codes generated in the page for the simulation — no real secrets are stored. A real build would need encrypted storage and a PIN lock, because the ESP32 has no secure element.

Live feed

Live BTC spot is asked from four independent sources in parallel — mempool.space, CoinGecko, Coinbase and Kraken — and a price is only shown once two sources agree, or it matches the last real close, so one blocked or bad feed can't freeze or fake the price. The site's own prices.json joins in only while its pipeline timestamp is under 15 minutes old. The chart's daily closes come from the site's data files extended by its history.json, Coinbase 15-minute candles power the 1D line, and mempool.space supplies block height, fees and mempool. Polls every 60 seconds, like the real keychain would over Wi-Fi. If a fetch fails, the last good data stays on screen and the feed status up top says so. Connecting…

What fits — and what doesn't

Scaled down from the full PaperStreet chart, keeping everything a 170-pixel-wide line chart can honestly show — and now wearing the site's own clothes.

  • ✓Now matches the site: the price screen mimics paperstreetbtc.com's price-only view and chart panel — cyan $ with the big white price, “BITCOIN · LIVE PRICE” label with the block number under it, USD $ / CAD $ pill, ▲ change · range · Updated line, cyan BTC line with $ labels down the left, and the active range pill in cyan instead of orange
  • ✓Now matches the site: the block clock mimics /clock/ — a focus card with the huge # block height and the glowing cyan ring filling toward the 10-minute target, rotating on its own through sats per dollar and the next halving (tap the dots to jump), over the site's small cards: sats per $10 / $100, difficulty adjustment, network fees
  • ✓Kept: live price, line chart with nine ranges — 1D / 1W / 1M / 3M / 6M / YTD / 1Y / 5Y / MAX — % change, range high & low, scrub readout (return only, while you drag). Longer ranges just ship fewer, wider-spaced points, which a line chart handles fine at this size
  • ✓Kept: comparisons — tap S&P, GOLD or MSTR under the chart to overlay that line, rebased to 100 at the start of the range like the full Compare chart, with its own % for the range on the chip. Any mix of the three, on any range
  • ✓New: price only — a whole swipe screen with nothing but the big price, its 24h change, sats per dollar and block height, plus light and dark themes for the screen (and this page) from the Theme switch in Display; System follows your device setting
  • ✓Kept: sats per dollar, block height, halving countdown, fees — the block-clock page
  • ✓Kept: Jade + Lightning receive QRs on swipe screens — static addresses, no keys on the device, ever
  • ✓New: landscape chart view — turn the keychain sideways (Landscape chart button by the device, or Orientation in Display) and the firmware redraws at 320 × 170: the chart stretches the full width at nearly double the portrait resolution, price and change share one row, and chart type, comparisons and all nine ranges share a single control row underneath. Scrubbing, candles and comparisons all keep working sideways; the portrait stat tiles give way to the wider chart. Turn it back to portrait for the other screens at their natural size
  • ✓New: Authenticator — a personal TOTP app on the last swipe screen: demo 2FA codes for PaperStreet, GitHub and email, refreshing every 30 s with a countdown bar; tap an account to enlarge its code, or hide the codes for privacy. This simulation stores no real secrets — a real build would need encrypted storage plus a PIN lock, since the ESP32 has no secure element
  • ✓Now trying — candles up to 1Y (this Space only): tap Candles under the chart. Candles are aggregated so each one stays readable at 170 px — 1D = 24 hourly, 1W = 28 × 6h, 1M = 30 daily, 3M = ~45, 6M / YTD / 1Y = 52 weekly candles (~2–3 px wide on 1Y). That reads surprisingly well for shape and big swings: green/red bodies and wicks are clear at 1.5×–2× zoom, tighter at actual size. Daily candles for a full year (365) would be sub-pixel noise, so 1Y uses weekly — and 5Y / MAX stay line-only for the same reason. Scrub still works in candle view (shows that candle's close + date); comparisons are line-only and grey out in candle view
  • ✕Left out: TA overlays — Bollinger, SMA/EMA and RSI need history windows and vertical room this screen doesn't have
